Privacy policy

This covers the Kitchen Stock web app (kitchen-stock.phone-liaison.workers.dev) and the Kitchen Stock phone app for Android and iPhone.

Effective October 2, 2026.

The short version
  • We keep your email address so you can sign in, and your kitchen list so it shows up on every phone you use.
  • No ads. No analytics or tracking. We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • Receipt photos are read on your phone. They are not uploaded.
  • The sample kitchen saves nothing.
  • Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we'll delete your account and data within 30 days.

Who we are

Kitchen Stock is made by Phil, a meat cutter in Tacoma, Washington. "We" and "us" in this policy means him. It is a small, independent app, not a company with a data business.

What stays on your phone

  • A copy of your kitchen. The app keeps a copy of your list on the device so it opens fast and works with no signal. Changes made offline wait on the phone until you're back online. If you use the app without signing in (web app only), your list lives only on that device.
  • Camera. The phone app uses the camera only to read barcodes, and only while the scanner is open. The web app can read a grocery receipt from a photo you take or pick; the text is read on your phone and the photo is never uploaded. The first time you read a receipt, the web app downloads the free text-reading program it needs (about 10 MB) from a public file server (jsDelivr). Only the download request goes out, not your photo.
  • Phone app reminders. In the Android and iPhone app, reminders are worked out and scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing about them is sent to us.
  • The sample kitchen. "Try the sample kitchen" uses made-up food and saves nothing, on your phone or anywhere else.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • We don't sell your data, rent it, or share it with anyone for their own use.
  • We don't read your kitchen list except when you ask us to help fix a problem, or if the law requires it.

We would share data if a court or law required it, and we would tell you if the law allowed us to.

Sharing a kitchen

When you share a kitchen, everyone in it can see and change its list and settings, and see each member's email address. Owners can invite and remove people. If you leave a kitchen, the list stays with the people still in it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and kitchen data while you use the app. If you ask us to delete your account, we do it within 30 days. Copies in the database provider's automatic backups may last a short time longer before they're overwritten. Items you delete in the app are removed from the database when your phone syncs.

Deleting your account and data

Today there is no delete-account button in the app. To delete your account, email [CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you sign in with and say "delete my account." We will delete it within 30 days and reply when it's done. That removes:

  • your sign-in and email address,
  • your reminder settings,
  • your membership in every kitchen, and
  • any kitchen where you are the only member, with everything in it.

A kitchen you share with others stays for them. If you want its contents gone too, say so, and since every member can edit it, an owner of that kitchen can clear it out first.

Things you can do yourself, right now:

  • Delete any item, person, flag or place in the app.
  • Leave a kitchen, or (as an owner) remove someone from it.
  • Turn reminders off in Settings.
  • Clear the copy on your phone by uninstalling the app, or by clearing this site's data in your browser. Signing out alone does not erase that copy.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email [CONTACT EMAIL]. We'll answer within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database only lets signed-in members of a kitchen read or change that kitchen. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we collect small so there's less to lose.

Children

Kitchen Stock is a household tool for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change matters, like collecting something new, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect.
