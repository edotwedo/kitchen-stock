What it does

Use first

Everything that's getting old, in one list, oldest first. Freezer dates carry the same day-dot colors restaurant kitchens use.

Shopping list that writes itself

Tap an item low or out and it lands on the list. Back from the store, tick what you bought and it's all full again.

Everyone's food rules

Add each person in the house and what they avoid or limit: pork, gluten, sugar, nuts, anything. Items get flagged so nobody has to remember.

One list, every phone

Share the kitchen with the people you live with. Changes show up on every phone, and it still works with no signal in the basement.

Scan it in

Point the camera at a barcode and the name, size and allergens fill themselves in from a free public food database. Only the barcode number is looked up.

Read the receipt

Back from the store, take a photo of the receipt. Things you already had go back to full and new things get added, after you check the list. The photo is read on your phone, never uploaded.

What can I cook?

Turns what you have, and what needs using first, into a recipe request you can paste into any AI chat.

Print it

Print the shopping list or a freezer sheet to stick on the door.

The freezer clock

Frozen food stays safe, but it doesn't stay good forever. Every freezer item gets a best-by date from how it's wrapped. You can change the numbers.

Put it on your phone

Kitchen Stock installs straight from the browser. It gets its own icon and opens like any other app.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app, then Install.
